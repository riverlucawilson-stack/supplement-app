## Level 1: human meta-analyses and RCTs

### Muscle and strength gains with resistance training

_section: What it can and can’t help with · not reviewed · moderate evidence_

A meta-analysis of 49 randomized trials (1,863 healthy adults doing resistance training for at least 6 weeks) found protein supplements added small extra gains: 2.49 kg more on a one-rep max, 0.30 kg more fat-free mass, and larger muscle fibers. The extra gain in fat-free mass was smaller in older people and larger in people already trained.
- One-rep max: +2.49 kg (95% CI 0.64 to 4.33)
- Fat-free mass: +0.30 kg (95% CI 0.09 to 0.52)

Sources: [1]

### Benefit levels off around 1.6 g/kg/day

_section: How much protein · not reviewed_

In the same meta-analysis, supplementing beyond a total protein intake of about 1.62 g per kg of body weight per day gave no further gain in fat-free mass. Total intake counts food and powder together.

Sources: [1]
