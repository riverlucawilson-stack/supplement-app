# Probiotics: how to compare them (proposal)

> Written by Claude 2026-11-05. NEEDS RIVER REVIEW. Nothing is built yet.

## The problem
Probiotic effects are specific to the strain (e.g. *Lactobacillus rhamnosus* GG is not the same as
another *L. rhamnosus*), so "more bacteria per dollar" isn't a fair way to rank products. CFU counts also
aren't comparable unless they're guaranteed the same way: "at time of manufacture" counts can drop
before you take them, while "through expiration" counts are promised for the product's shelf life.

## Options
| Option | How it ranks | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| A. Cost per daily serving, facts beside it (**recommended**) | Price ÷ servings, like multivitamins and B-complex | Doesn't say which product is "more probiotic", and that's deliberate |
| B. Cost per billion CFU | Price ÷ total CFU | Rewards big CFU numbers; mixes "at manufacture" with "through expiration"; ignores strains |
| C. Compare only within a strain | One table per strain | Most strains would have one or two products, so there's little to compare |

## Recommendation: option A
- Sort by **cost per daily serving** as the label defines it (the method you approved for multivitamins).
- Show beside it, never scored:
  - every strain with its **full designation** as printed (genus, species, and strain code, e.g. "*Lactobacillus rhamnosus* GG"), or a flag when the label gives no strain code;
  - **CFU per serving** as labeled;
  - whether the count is **guaranteed through expiration**, **at manufacture**, or **not stated**;
  - **storage** as labeled (refrigerate, or shelf-stable);
  - number of strains.
- A filter for "guaranteed through expiration" and one for "shelf-stable".
- Products whose label lists no CFU, or lists strains only inside a proprietary blend without
  amounts, are still included but flagged.
- No adjustment for claims such as delayed release or "survives stomach acid": record the claim and its source.
